# Cancellation Help

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order **free of charge any time before it is Shipped**. Go to **My Orders → select order → Cancel Order**, choose a reason and confirm.

- If you paid online, the refund is started immediately (see Refunds Help for timelines).
- If you chose Cash on Delivery, nothing is charged.

**After an order is Shipped, it cannot be cancelled.** You can refuse the package at the doorstep, or accept it and request a return if the item is eligible.

## Cancellation fees for orders
There is **no cancellation fee** for cancelling an order before it ships.

If you cancel or refuse Cash on Delivery orders **3 or more times within 30 days**, Cash on Delivery may be disabled on your account for 60 days.

## Cancelling a ShopNest Plus membership
You can cancel ShopNest Plus from **Profile → ShopNest Plus → Manage Membership → Cancel**.

- **Within 7 days of purchase or renewal:** full refund, as long as you have not used any Plus benefit.
- **Annual plan, after 7 days:** you get a pro-rated refund for the remaining full months, minus an **early termination fee of ₹99**.
- **Monthly plan:** no refund; your benefits continue until the end of the current month and the plan will not renew.

## Order cancelled by ShopNest
Rarely, an order may be cancelled by us or the seller (for example, the item is out of stock or the address is not serviceable). In that case you get a full refund automatically and an SMS/email explaining the reason.
