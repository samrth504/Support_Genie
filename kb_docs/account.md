# Account Help

## Creating an account
You can create a ShopNest account with your email address or your mobile number. Open the app or website, tap **Sign Up**, enter your name, email or mobile number and a password, then verify the 6-digit OTP we send you. The OTP is valid for 10 minutes. One mobile number can be linked to only one ShopNest account.

## Resetting or changing your password
If you forgot your password, tap **Forgot Password** on the login screen and enter your registered email or mobile number. We will send a password reset link that is valid for **30 minutes**. If the link has expired, request a new one.

To change your password while logged in, go to **Profile → Settings → Security → Change Password**. You will need your current password. For your safety, all other devices are logged out after a password change.

If you do not receive the reset email, check your spam folder and make sure you are using the email registered with ShopNest.

## Editing your profile
Go to **Profile → Edit Profile** to change your name, email address, mobile number, date of birth or profile photo. Changing your email or mobile number requires OTP verification on the new email or number.

## Two-step verification
You can turn on two-step verification in **Profile → Settings → Security**. When it is on, we ask for an OTP every time you log in on a new device.

## Switching between accounts
The ShopNest app supports up to 3 saved accounts on one device. Go to **Profile → Switch Account** to add or switch accounts without logging out.

## Deleting your account
Go to **Profile → Settings → Privacy → Delete Account**. Before deleting:
- All open orders must be delivered, cancelled or returned.
- Any ShopNest Wallet balance is lost after deletion, so use it first.
- An active ShopNest Plus membership is cancelled without refund.

After you confirm, your account is deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after a **14-day grace period**. If you log in again within 14 days, the deletion is cancelled.

## Account locked
After 5 wrong password attempts, your account is locked for 30 minutes. You can unlock it immediately by resetting your password.
