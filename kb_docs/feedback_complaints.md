# Feedback, Reviews and Complaints

## Writing a product review
You can review a product **after it is delivered**: **My Orders → select order → Rate & Review**. Reviews can include a 1–5 star rating, text and up to 5 photos. Reviews are published within 24 hours after moderation.

Reviews are removed if they contain abusive language, personal information, links, or content unrelated to the product.

## Editing or deleting a review
Go to **Profile → My Reviews** to edit or delete any review you have written.

## Giving feedback about ShopNest
We read every suggestion. Go to **Profile → Help Center → Share Feedback** to tell us about the app, delivery experience or customer support. After every support conversation you also get a short 1-question survey.

## Filing a complaint
If you are unhappy with a product, a seller, a delivery partner or our support:
1. Go to **Help Center → Raise a Ticket → File a Complaint**.
2. Describe the issue, and add the order ID and photos if relevant.
3. You get a ticket number immediately by SMS and email.

Complaints are resolved within **3 working days**. If you are not satisfied with the resolution, you can escalate it to the Grievance Officer (see Contact Customer Support).

## Complaints about seller behaviour
If a seller sent a counterfeit, used or misdescribed product, report it with photos. Confirmed cases lead to a full refund for you and action against the seller.
