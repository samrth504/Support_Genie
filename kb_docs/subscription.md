# Subscriptions Help — ShopNest Plus and Newsletter

## What is ShopNest Plus?
ShopNest Plus is our paid membership. Plans:
- **Annual:** ₹999 per year
- **Monthly:** ₹129 per month

Benefits:
- Free Express delivery on all orders
- Early access to sales (24 hours before everyone else)
- 5% cashback to ShopNest Wallet on every order (maximum ₹200 per order)
- Priority customer support

## Joining ShopNest Plus
Go to **Profile → ShopNest Plus → Join Now**, choose a plan and pay with any online payment method. Cash on Delivery cannot be used for memberships. Benefits start immediately.

## Renewal
Plans renew automatically using the saved payment method. We send a reminder 3 days before renewal. You can turn off auto-renewal any time from **Profile → ShopNest Plus → Manage Membership**.

For cancelling ShopNest Plus and refund rules, see the Cancellation Help page.

## Newsletter and promotional emails
- **Subscribe:** Profile → Settings → Notifications → turn on "Offers and Newsletter".
- **Unsubscribe:** turn the same setting off, or click **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of any newsletter email.

Changes take effect within **48 hours**. Unsubscribing from the newsletter does not stop important emails about your orders, payments and account security.

## SMS and WhatsApp notifications
You can choose which order updates you receive by SMS or WhatsApp in **Profile → Settings → Notifications**.
