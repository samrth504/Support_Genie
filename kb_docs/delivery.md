# Delivery Help

## Delivery options and charges
| Option | Delivery time | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | 3–7 business days | Free on orders of ₹499 or more, otherwise ₹40 |
| **Express** | 1–2 business days (metro cities) | ₹99 (free for ShopNest Plus members) |
| **Same-Day** | Same day, for orders placed before 12 PM | ₹149 |

**Same-Day delivery** is available in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Pune and Hyderabad only.

The estimated delivery date is shown on the product page after you enter your pincode, and again at checkout.

## Checking if delivery is available
Enter your 6-digit pincode on any product page. If the pincode is not serviceable, the product cannot be delivered to that address. ShopNest delivers only within India.

## Delivery attempts
The delivery partner makes up to **3 delivery attempts**. If all attempts fail, the package is returned to the seller and a refund is issued for prepaid orders.

## Delayed delivery
Deliveries may be delayed because of weather, festivals, strikes or high order volumes. You can see the latest status in **My Orders**. If your order has not been delivered **3 days after the estimated delivery date**, contact support with your order ID.

## Package marked delivered but not received
Check with family members, neighbours and your building security first. If you still cannot find it, report it within **48 hours** from **My Orders → Report an Issue → Not received**. We investigate with the courier within 3 working days.

## Open-box delivery
For mobiles and laptops above ₹15,000, the delivery partner opens the box in front of you so you can check the product before accepting it.
