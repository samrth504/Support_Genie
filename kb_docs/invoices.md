# Invoices Help

## Downloading an invoice
An invoice is available once your order is **Shipped**. Go to **My Orders → select order → Download Invoice**. The invoice is a PDF and is also emailed to your registered email address after delivery.

If an order has items from multiple sellers, each seller issues a separate invoice.

## GST invoice for business purchases
To get a GST invoice with your company details, add your **GSTIN and company name at checkout** before placing the order (tick "Use GST invoice"). **A GSTIN cannot be added after the order is placed.**

## Invoice details are wrong
If the name or address on the invoice is wrong, you can request a correction within **30 days of delivery** from **My Orders → select order → Report an Issue → Invoice correction**. Corrections take up to 3 working days. The GSTIN itself cannot be changed after the order is placed.

## Invoice for cancelled or returned orders
No invoice is generated for orders cancelled before shipping. For returned orders, a **credit note** is issued and can be downloaded from the same order page after the refund is completed.

## Lost invoice email
You can always download invoices for orders from the last 2 years from **My Orders**, even if you deleted the email.
