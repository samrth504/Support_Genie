# Contact Customer Support

## Ways to reach us
| Channel | Availability | Typical response time |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Help Chat** (in app / website) | 24 × 7 | Instant |
| **Support ticket** (Help Center → Raise a Ticket) | 24 × 7 | Within 24 hours |
| **Email:** support@shopnest.in | 24 × 7 | Within 24 hours |
| **Phone helpline:** 1800-000-1234 (toll-free) | Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–9 PM IST | Live agent |

ShopNest Plus members get priority support: tickets are answered within 6 hours.

## Talking to a human agent
In the AI Help Chat, type **"talk to an agent"** at any time. Your conversation is transferred to a human agent during working hours (Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–9 PM IST). Outside working hours, a ticket is created and an agent replies the next working day.

## What to keep ready
- Your order ID (for example SN-12345678)
- The registered email or mobile number
- Screenshots or photos, if the issue is about a damaged or wrong item
- Transaction ID / UTR for payment issues

## Escalation and grievance officer
If your issue is not resolved within **3 working days** of raising a ticket, you can escalate it to our Grievance Officer at **grievance@shopnest.in**. Include your ticket number. The Grievance Officer responds within **48 hours**.

## Beware of fraud
ShopNest's only official helpline is **1800-000-1234**. We never ask for your OTP, CVV, UPI PIN or password, and we never ask you to install screen-sharing apps.
