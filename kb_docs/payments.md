# Payments Help

## Accepted payment methods
ShopNest accepts:
- **UPI** (any UPI app)
- **Credit and debit cards** – Visa, Mastercard and RuPay
- **Net banking** from all major Indian banks
- **ShopNest Wallet**
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)** – for orders up to **₹10,000**. A COD handling fee of **₹29** applies to orders below ₹499.

You can save cards securely for faster checkout. ShopNest never stores your card CVV.

## Payment failed but money was debited
Sometimes a payment fails but the amount is debited from your account. This usually happens because of a bank or network delay.
- If no order was created, the money is **automatically reversed by your bank within 5–7 working days**.
- If the order shows **Payment Pending**, wait 30 minutes; it often confirms automatically.
- Do not pay again for the same order until the status is clear, to avoid a double charge.

If the money is not back after 7 working days, contact support with your **UTR / transaction ID** from your bank statement.

## Payment declined
A payment can be declined because of insufficient balance, wrong OTP, card limits or the bank's security checks. Try another payment method or contact your bank.

## ShopNest Wallet
The ShopNest Wallet holds refunds and cashback. Wallet money can be used on any order but cannot be transferred to a bank account, except for refunds of Cash on Delivery orders. Wallet balance does not expire as long as your account is active.

## Payment security
All payments are processed over encrypted connections by RBI-authorised payment partners. ShopNest staff will **never** ask for your OTP, CVV, UPI PIN or password by phone, email or chat. If someone asks, it is a fraud — report it to support.
