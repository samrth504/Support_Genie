# Refunds and Returns Help

## Return window
Most items can be returned within **10 days of delivery**. Electronics (mobiles, laptops, headphones, smart watches) can be returned within **7 days of delivery** and are eligible for replacement or refund only after a quality check.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Innerwear, socks and swimwear
- Perishable items such as groceries and flowers
- Personalised or custom-made products
- Digital products and software licences
- Items marked **Non-returnable** on the product page

## How to request a return
1. Go to **My Orders**, select the delivered order and tap **Return**.
2. Choose the reason and upload photos if the item is damaged or wrong.
3. Choose a pickup slot. Our courier picks up the item from your address.

Keep the item unused, with original tags and packaging. A quality check is done within **48 hours of pickup**.

## Refund timelines
The refund starts after the quality check is passed (or immediately for cancelled orders). Time taken for the money to reach you:

| Original payment method | Refund time |
|---|---|
| UPI | 1–3 working days |
| ShopNest Wallet | Instant |
| Credit / debit card | 5–7 working days |
| Net banking | 5–7 working days |
| Cash on Delivery | Instant to ShopNest Wallet, or 5–7 working days to your bank account (you choose) |

You can track the refund in **My Orders → select order → Refund Status**. If the refund is marked "Completed" but not visible, check with your bank using the Refund Reference Number shown there.

## Charged twice for the same order
If you were charged twice for one order, the duplicate payment is automatically reversed to your original payment method within **5–7 working days**. If it is not reversed after 7 working days, contact support with the transaction ID from your bank statement.

## Refund policy for partial returns
If you return only some items from an order, you are refunded only for those items. Delivery charges are refunded only if the entire order is returned because of a damaged, defective or wrong item.
